Privacy policy

Personal Drive CLI. Effective 17 October 2026.

Who uses it

Personal Drive CLI is used only by its owner, Nithin Datt, on his own Google accounts. No one else signs in to it.

What it reads and changes

With the owner's consent, given on Google's own sign-in screen, it uses Google APIs to read and change:

  • files in the owner's Google Drive;
  • the owner's YouTube account data: subscriptions, playlists and the videos in them, ratings, and public video and channel details.

How the data is used

Only to organise the owner's own accounts, when he asks. The owner may use AI assistants of his choice on his computer to do this work for him.

Where it is kept

The data stays in the owner's Google accounts and on his own computer. The sign-in tokens are stored only on his computer. There is no server and no database.

Sharing

The data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone.

Personal Drive CLI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

Personal Drive CLI uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time on the Google account's third-party connections page. Removing it also stops all YouTube access. Deleting the tokens on the owner's computer has the same effect locally.

Through the owner's GitHub profile: github.com/nithindatt.
